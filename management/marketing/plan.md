# Marketing Plan

**Brand:** Keratin Glow Bahrain  
**Channels:** Website, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps / Ads  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-11

## Positioning
Premium hair salon in **Janabiyah, Bahrain** specializing in keratin, nanoplasty, botox/protein, and color (balayage). Booking via **WhatsApp**.

## Objectives
| Objective | Metric |
|-----------|--------|
| More bookings | WhatsApp conversations / week |
| Local visibility | Maps / Search impressions for keratin Bahrain |
| Trust | Reviews + before/after engagement |
| Brand consistency | Same NAP, hours, logo everywhere |

## Target audience
- Women in Bahrain / GCC seeking keratin or strong straightening
- Clients with curly / Afro / frizzy hair needing nanoplasty
- Color clients (balayage, roots, blond)

## Channel strategy
| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---------|------|---------|
| Website | Convert + SEO | Keep updated; gallery + hours accurate |
| WhatsApp | Primary booking | Reply fast; use pre-filled CTAs |
| Instagram @keratinglow_bh | Proof + reach | 3–5 posts/Reels per week |
| Google Business Profile | Local pack | Weekly posts + ask for reviews |
| Google Ads | Demand capture | Test search: “keratin Bahrain” |

## Message pillars
1. Real results (before/after)
2. Expert keratin / nanoplasty care
3. Easy WhatsApp booking
4. Located in Janabiyah — easy to find

## Campaign ideas
1. **Gallery launch** — post real transformations + link to `#results`
2. **Review drive** — happy clients → Google review link after treatment
3. **Seasonal hair** — summer humidity / keratin offer
4. **Arabic creatives** — captions + site language later

## Offer / CTA rules
- Primary CTA always: **Book on WhatsApp**
- Secondary: **Find us on Maps** / **Follow Instagram**
- Never bury the phone number

## Brand assets
- Logo: `public/logo.webp`, `public/hero-logo.webp`
- OG share image: `public/og-image.webp`
- Colors: black + gold (`#d4af37`)

## Monthly review
- What content drove WhatsApp?
- Which services were asked about most?
- Ads: spend vs bookings
- Update content calendar for next month
